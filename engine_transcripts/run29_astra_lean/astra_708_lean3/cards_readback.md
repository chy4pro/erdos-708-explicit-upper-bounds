large_atoms: For any atom system and m ≥ 4096, on 1 ≤ k ≤ m the large part is at most 1 and the threshold-65 hinge is bounded by the small threshold-64 hinge; the small right hinge is bounded by the full right hinge everywhere.
dense: If the mean of the atoms with 64 p^j ≤ m is at least 17/16, the threshold-65 full-atom hinge is dominated by the window threshold-1 hinge; the same holds for additive weights with that small-atom mean.
hinge_65_of_sparse_core: The exact Section-14 sparse-core inequality implies the threshold-65 additive hinge inequality for all finite prime weights in [0,1], positive lengths, and nonnegative window starts.
dual: The fractional cover infimum equals the nonnegative dual maximum, attained by coefficients in [0,1] on the primes dividing the input product.
rounding: A subset of the window covers the input product and has cardinality at most the fractional optimum plus the number of primes dividing that product.
few_primes: If the maximum input is less than 8 times the cube of its cardinality, fewer than 16 times that cardinality primes divide the input product.
long_interval: If the maximum input is at least 8 times the cube of its cardinality, a subset of at most twice its cardinality covers the product in every window.
conditional_81: The universal threshold-65 additive hinge inequality implies a covering subset of size at most 81 times the input cardinality.
linear_bound: Assuming the exact independent sparse-core inequality, every nonempty n-element set of integers ≥ 2 has, in every window of length max A, a covering subset of at most 81n integers.
